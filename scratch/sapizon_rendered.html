Current Openings

Job Type: Full-time

Position: Sales Consultant (Direct Sales)

Experience: 5+ Years

Education: Any

Location: Any

Job Description:

Role Overview:

As a Sales Consultant, you will play a pivotal role in driving revenue growth and expanding our client base. You will be responsible for identifying and pursuing new business opportunities, cultivating relationships with prospective clients, and promoting our XR solutions to meet their needs. You will also collaborate closely with our development and marketing team to ensure that our offerings remain competitive and aligned with market trends.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Identify and prospect new business opportunities in target markets and industries.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with key decision-makers and stakeholders.
  • Conduct product demonstrations and presentations to showcase the value and capabilities of our XR solutions.
  • Collaborate with internal teams to develop customized solutions and proposals that address client needs and requirements.
  • Negotiate contracts and close deals to achieve sales targets and objectives.
  • Stay updated on industry trends, competitive landscape, and emerging technologies to inform sales strategies and approaches.

Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Marketing, or a related field.
  • Proven track record of success in sales or business growth, preferably in IT/Software industry.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex concepts in a clear and compelling manner.
  • Self-motivated and results-oriented, with a demonstrated ability to work independently and drive initiatives forward.
  • Knowledge of XR (AR & VR) technologies and their applications across various industries (preferred).
  • Willingness to travel as needed to meet with clients and attend industry events.

Why Join Us?

  • Opportunity to be at the forefront of innovation with cutting-edge technologies.
  • Competitive compensation package, including base salary, and performance perks.
  • Collaborative and inclusive work environment that values creativity, diversity, and continuous learning.
  • Career growth and development opportunities as we expand and scale our operations.
